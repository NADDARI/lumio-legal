Privacy — Lumio

Lumio is an M3U and Xtream stream player. The app has no account and no server operated by the publisher.

Data that stays on the device

  • The playlist address (often including a credential) is stored in the device Keychain, without iCloud sync.
  • The source name, the host, favorites, and settings stay in the app’s local storage.
  • This data is not sent to the publisher.

Network

When you add a playlist or play a channel, the app contacts only the servers in the address you provided (the list, the stream, and logos). The publisher does not receive those requests.

Tracking

No advertising tracking, no analytics, and no ads.
